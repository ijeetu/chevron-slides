Slide 1
Registration Process
Adult Path
Adult 18 or older: continue with direct registration.
Minor Path
Minor accounts require a linked parent or guardian.
Parent controls the account preferences for the minor.
Required Inputs
Display name: real name, business name, or pseudonym.
Email address or phone number: used to send a unique verification code.
Date of birth: used strictly for age assurance.
Account Setup
Password: secure, unique, minimum 8 characters.
Username: unique @handle not already in use.
Optional Profile
Profile picture or avatar.
Bio with up to 160 characters.
Interests or topics to shape the initial timeline.
Contacts syncing permission to find known people.

Slide 2
Validation Process
Status Choice
Non-citizen: standard non-citizen path.
Citizen: continue to third-party validation.
Citizen Validation
If citizen, hand off to third-party validation through ID.me.
ID.me resolves standard cases through document upload.
Trusted Referee video call handles exceptions or edge cases.
Identity Proof
U.S. driver license, state ID, passport, or passport card.
Full name, date of birth, phone, email, address, and Social Security Number.
Biometric Match
Selfie compared against the government-issued ID.
Mobile network operator data to verify phone ownership.
Geolocation captured at time of verification.
Status Specific Proof
Student, military, nurse, or other role-based validation may require an ID card, pay stub, or enrollment record.
